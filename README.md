# 🌩️ मौसम AI

## AI-Powered Hyperlocal Convective Storm Nowcasting & Early Warning System

> **Predict • Prepare • Protect**

मौसम AI is an AI-powered hyperlocal weather intelligence and early-warning platform designed for **convective storm nowcasting across India**.

The platform is designed to combine weather observations from multiple sources such as **Doppler Weather Radar, satellite data, lightning detection, ground weather stations and IIoT sensors** to provide localized storm intelligence for the next **0–6 hours**.

The core objective is to move beyond simply displaying weather information and provide actionable intelligence:

**Sense → Predict → Understand → Act**

---

## 🚨 Problem Statement

Convective weather events such as thunderstorms, lightning, hailstorms, heavy rainfall, cloudbursts and strong winds can develop rapidly and create risks for people, agriculture, industries and critical infrastructure.

Existing weather information may not always provide sufficient hyperlocal information about:

- Where a storm is moving
- How fast it is moving
- When it may reach a specific location
- Which hazards may occur
- Which critical assets may be affected
- What precautionary action should be considered

This creates a gap between:

**Weather Prediction → Local Impact → Action**

मौसम AI is designed to bridge this gap through an integrated weather intelligence platform.

---

# 💡 Proposed Solution

मौसम AI combines:

- Multi-source weather data
- Storm detection
- Storm tracking
- Digital Twin visualization
- Hazard intelligence
- Impact prediction
- ETA estimation
- AI-based risk scoring
- Explainable warnings
- IIoT sensor integration
- Risk-to-action intelligence

The platform provides a single dashboard for monitoring evolving convective storms and understanding their possible local impact.

---

# 🎯 Objectives

The major objectives of मौसम AI are:

1. Provide 0–6 hour convective storm nowcasting
2. Provide 1–3 km hyperlocal weather intelligence
3. Fuse radar, satellite, lightning and ground observations
4. Track storm movement in real time
5. Create a Storm Digital Twin
6. Estimate storm arrival time at important locations
7. Identify lightning, hail, wind and rainfall hazards
8. Generate an explainable AI risk score
9. Integrate hyperlocal IIoT sensors
10. Monitor sensor-network health
11. Connect weather risk with operational actions
12. Support scalable deployment across India
